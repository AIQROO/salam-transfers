Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about your private transfers in Cancun and Riviera Maya.

Are the transfers private or shared? +

All of our transfers are private. You do not share the vehicle with other passengers — only with your family or your group.

Do you only serve Muslim clients? +

No. Salam Transfers is open to any traveler who prefers a respectful and peaceful environment.

Can you take me to the mosque on Friday? +

Yes. We offer dedicated transportation to the mosques Masjid Al-Rahim (Cancun) and Masjid Al-Rahman (Playa del Carmen), with waiting service during prayer.

How does payment work? +

You confirm your booking with a deposit paid online. The remaining balance is paid directly to the driver on the day of service in cash, card, or bank transfer.

Is the deposit refundable? +

No. The deposit secures your reservation and is non-refundable. However, you can change the date or time at no cost with at least 24 hours' notice, subject to availability.

Do you offer full-day service for tours? +

Yes. We offer a private driver by the hour, half-day, or full-day for tours, parks, cenotes, shopping, or any itinerary you choose.

Can you coordinate transportation for a wedding or event? +

Yes. We coordinate transportation for destination weddings, Islamic events, and conferences. Request a personalized quote.

What happens if my flight is delayed? +

We monitor your flight in real time and adjust the pickup time at no additional cost within a reasonable window.

Do you have child seats? +

Yes, upon prior request. Please indicate this when booking and we will confirm availability.

Do you speak English, French, or Arabic? +

Our team handles reservations in Spanish and English. For services in French or Arabic, please write to us in advance and we will coordinate.

How can I pay the balance to the driver? +

We accept cash (MXN or USD), credit/debit card, or bank transfer.

Do I need to book in advance? +

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance to guarantee availability, especially during peak season.

Do the rates include tolls? +

Some destinations do, others do not. This is clearly indicated in the pricing table. Tolls not included are paid directly to the driver during the trip.
