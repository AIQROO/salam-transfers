Cancellation & Change Policies

Booking Deposit

To guarantee the reservation and secure the vehicle with its assigned driver, a partial deposit is charged online. This deposit is non-refundable if the client cancels the service unilaterally.

Free Date Changes

We understand that travel plans can change. We allow modifications to transfer dates and times without penalties up to 24 hours before the scheduled service time.

Delay Courtesy

If your flight is delayed, don't worry. We monitor your flight status constantly. Your driver will wait for you free of charge for as long as necessary after your actual landing.

If you need additional transportation during your visit — tours, sightseeing, mosque transfers, or airport return — contact us via WhatsApp and we will assign the available driver. Booking through Salam Transfers guarantees 24/7 bilingual support, a formal invoice if required, and backing in case of any unforeseen circumstances.
