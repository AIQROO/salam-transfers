Thoughtful Details

At Salam Transfers, we are proud to be the only private transportation service in the Mexican Caribbean specialized in welcoming the international Muslim travel community with deep respect.

Our mission is to make your stay in Cancun and the Riviera Maya completely pleasant and worry-free, allowing you to enjoy the beauties of the Mexican Caribbean without compromising your daily spiritual values and practices.

Salat Support (Prayer times)

Our drivers are fully aware of daily prayer schedules and have mapped clean, quiet locations along the highways for prayer stops. Clean prayer mats are available inside the vehicle upon request.

Simply let your driver know when it is prayer time, and he will gladly coordinate a suitable stop at a nearby mosque or a quiet, clean spot along the way.

Pristine Family Environment

We guarantee 100% alcohol-free, smoke-free, and odor-free vehicles. A peaceful and respectful environment for your loved ones.

We firmly believe in customer respect. Our vans are rigorously sanitized after each service, maintaining a clean, neutral, and comfortable environment for you and your children.
